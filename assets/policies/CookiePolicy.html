Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones GrowApp uses, and how you can control them. We keep our cookie footprint minimal — we're a grow app, not an ad network.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you interact with it. Cookies don't contain your grow data — that lives in IndexedDB on your device.

Cookies can be Session (deleted when you close your browser) or Persistent (remain until they expire or you delete them).

Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Kind Purpose
Essential Session Required for core site functionality. Without these, certain features won't work.
Preference Persistent Remembers your choices — like view mode (cards vs. table) and plant size filter — so you don't have to reset them every visit.
Analytics Persistent Google Analytics cookies that help us understand which features are used most. Data is anonymized and aggregated.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell cookie data to third parties.

localStorage & IndexedDB

In addition to cookies, GrowApp uses localStorage (for UI preferences) and IndexedDB (for your grow schedules, diary entries, and check-in data). These are stored entirely on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some site functionality. Here's how to manage cookies in common browsers:

To opt out of Google Analytics specifically, use Google's opt-out browser add-on.
