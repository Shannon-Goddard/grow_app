Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

At Loyal9 LLC, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use GrowApp, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain — no legalese walls.

What We Collect

Data You Create

GrowApp stores your grow data — strain selections, feeding schedules, daily check-ins, diary photos, and notes — locally on your device using IndexedDB and localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Usage Data

When you use the Service, we may automatically collect standard usage information such as your device type, browser version, IP address, pages visited, and time spent. This helps us understand how the app is used and where to improve it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics. Google Analytics may set cookies on your device. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Service
  • To understand usage patterns and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To send important updates about the Service (if you've opted in)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your grow data for advertising.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies — primarily for analytics and remembering your preferences (like view mode and plant size). See our Cookie Policy for details.

Data Sharing

We only share data in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted third parties (like Google Analytics) that help us operate the Service, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • Business transfers — if Loyal9 LLC is acquired or merges, your data may transfer as part of that transaction. We'll notify you beforehand.

Data Retention

Your grow data lives on your device and is under your control — delete the app or clear your browser storage to remove it. Any server-side usage data we hold is retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by contacting us. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

GrowApp is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.

Third-Party Links

The Service may link to external sites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices — review their policies before sharing any information with them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date when we do. For significant changes, we'll post a notice in the app or on the website.

Questions?
