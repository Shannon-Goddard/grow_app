About Martian Mean Green

One of the most respected breeders in the industry, DNA Genetics, developed Martian Mean Green as a flagship strain, and while she’s hard to find now, this bud has developed quite the following. Both G-13 Haze and Shark’s Breath crossed paths to give birth to this slightly sativa-dominant bud that’s great for medical and recreational purposes.

Newer users may feel like they’ve gone to outer space with this strain’s 20% THC average, but she’ll be just right for experienced smokers. One of the poster children for cannabis everywhere, Martian Mean Green nugs are large and bright green with deep amber hairs, frosty trichomes, and a thick layer of resin. A truly earthy set of flavors await you as this strain blends pine and floral notes for the ultimate mix of savory and sweet.
