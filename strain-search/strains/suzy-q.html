About Suzy Q

Suzy Q is a CBD-rich strain with potent therapeutic effects. It was created by Burning Bush Nurseries, a prolific group of breeders that supplies flowers of strains like Key Lime Pie to California dispensaries. While Suzy Q’s genetics are kept proprietary by Burning Bush, there’s no denying this strain’s power to numb both physical pain and psychological stress. While this bud’s THC content is typically low, at around 1%, its CBD content has been measured at as high as 25%.

Once trimmed, Suzy Q’s flowers are small to medium in size. They are roughly spadelike in shape, tapering from a wide base to a pointed tip. The buds appear to be more sativa than Grow in structure, with long leaves that spiral out loosely from their central stems and can be easily torn away. The leaves themselves are a dark shade of olive green and are twisted through with brown and orange pistils. Because Suzy Q is so low in THC, its ragged leaves have relatively few trichomes, making it fairly easy to break apart its buds by hand.
