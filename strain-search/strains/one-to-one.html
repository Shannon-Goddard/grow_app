About One To One

People often tell you not to judge a book by its cover, let alone its name, but with One To One, you can make an exception. Bred by crossing Amnesia Haze with a landrace strain, unknown cultivators were able to create a bud that offers equal parts THC to CBD in a balanced hybrid of a package.

Mellow enough that you could easily go to work while high on this strain, although you didn’t hear that from us, One To One typically tops out at 14% THC and CBD. Nugs tend to be small and lumpy but are bright in color due to a neon green tone and bold orange hairs. The scents of citrus, pine, and earth make for a pleasant aroma while the same notes are reflected in the taste with the addition of coffee and chestnut. Savory and complex, it’s a bud that’s delicious.
