About Great White Shark

Great White Shark is a powerful Grow dominant hybrid strain of cannabis created by breeder The Green House. It is a recipient of several awards, including the 1997 High Times Cannabis Cup and 1st place prize in the 2005 High Life Hemp Fair, making it an elder strain with a storied history. It is a cross breed of Super Skunk with strains from Brazil and southern Indian. The THC levels of the plant hover around 14% to 15%.

Great White Shark has a sweet, earthy scent with citrus undertones. It is mildly tropical in both scent and taste. The aroma isn't overpowering or pungent, making Great White Shark manageable in the odor department. The smooth smoke has a mild taste, a mix of both fruit and skunk with a slight tang.
