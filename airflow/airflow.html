Get Your Airflow Right

Proper airflow is the difference between a healthy grow and a mold disaster. Calculate your CFM needs, then shop inline fan + carbon filter kits from the top brands.

Why Airflow Matters

Your grow tent is a sealed environment. Without active ventilation, heat builds up, humidity spikes, CO₂ depletes, and stale air creates the perfect conditions for mold, mildew, and pests. A properly sized inline fan solves all of this at once.

The Rule: 1× Air Exchange Per Minute

The standard target is to replace all the air in your tent once every minute. That means your fan's CFM rating should equal (or exceed) your tent's cubic footage. Add 25% overhead for a carbon filter, ducting bends, and future-proofing.

Duct Size Guide

  • 4" duct — 2×2 to 3×3 tents, up to ~250 CFM
  • 6" duct — 3×3 to 4×4 tents, 300–450 CFM sweet spot
  • 8" duct — 4×4 to 5×5+ tents, 700+ CFM for large spaces

Digital vs. Knob Controllers

Digital controllers (GrowHub, MarsPro APP, speed dial) let you set exact fan speeds, program schedules, and respond to temperature/humidity sensors. Knob controllers are simpler and cheaper — manual speed adjustment, no automation. For most growers, digital is worth the extra cost.

Carbon Filter Sizing

Your carbon filter must match your fan's duct size. A 6" fan needs a 6" filter. Mismatched sizes reduce airflow and odor control. All kits below include a matched filter — no guesswork needed.
